# Editable character animation

Choose the drawing language and acting for the brief: frame-by-frame drawings, SVG cutouts, Blender Grease Pencil, a3D rig or another native character tool can all be appropriate. Lip sync is one layer. Stage a readable intention, pose, gaze, anticipation and response; mouth changes alone are not a complete performance.

## Grounded native 3D action

For steps, reaching or pushing, begin with the action and support contacts before choosing a surface style. The installed Blender route supports native armatures, editable control keys and two-bone IK, inverse kinematics that rotates a limb to reach a target. Keep root/pelvis motion separate from world-space contact targets. Set chain length deliberately; zero includes every ancestor. A small segmented character is appropriate for some briefs; organic deformation, detailed anatomy or captured motion need their own rig and evidence.

Check both the evaluated bones and the visible meshes. An armature modifier alone does not guarantee that rig-object motion carries the mesh correctly. Preserve the intended parent transforms and binding, then measure actual evaluated geometry against its bone. The Pip proof found a detached-mesh defect despite accurate IK endpoints; correct binding fixed the visible limbs. Avoid masking that failure with a camera or treating control coordinates as proof of geometry.

Measure stance soles against the ground, limb reach, hand-to-prop separation and the visible support transfer across transitions. Keep authored motion distinct from a physical simulation. Place props outside one another's actual bounds; an attractive projection can hide an intersection. Inspect the action at output scale and its rest poses, not only constraint errors.

Choose cadence independently from materials. For digital clay/stop-motion looks, hold the whole action clock coherently, including root, IK targets, poles and contacted props. Stepping only deform bones leaves live targets moving and may cause sliding. Keep the smooth performance recoverable. A stepped 3D render is not physical stop-motion capture or hand-drawn cel animation.

For a cel-shaded 3D look, EEVEE Shader To RGB and a discrete Color Ramp can separate lighting into bands. Review the Combined image; the shader is not equivalent in Cycles, separated render passes or a generic glTF material. Preserve native `.blend` materials and actions. Treat hand-drawn cels as another medium; use actual drawings/Grease Pencil or consider a current dedicated drawing tool when the brief earns it.

Blender 5.1 actions use slots/layers/channel bags. Inspect the installed API rather than copying old `action.fcurves` code. Retain assigned actions and deliberately preserve unused alternatives with Fake User. Reopen the selected native file and verify active actions, control state, repeated/reverse seeks and relevant contacts. A small pose revision can be saved in a separate native copy and render only its affected range; source/settings changes must not silently reuse unrelated cached pixels. Exact cache identity or comparison evidence is needed before reusing frames.

The original task's `outputs/studio/unified-skill/character-motion-proof/` retains the Pip action, its build/render/check source and separate clay/cel native files. Use its current README/receipt for selected versions and commands. It is a compact example of one authored performance with different treatments, not a default character, style, universal rig or claim of every animation technique.

An actual ending-only revision reused63 of72 original clay images and rendered9 changed exposures. An additional unaffected control image matched decoded RGB exactly. Its PNG file hash differed because Blender embeds source path, date and render duration; separate content comparisons from file-identity checks. Keep both identities when proving revision reuse, and never infer arbitrary cache validity from one matching image.

## Organic skinning and expressive shapes

For a soft creature, use actual connected surfaces with blended joint weights, appropriate rest topology and native deformation controls. A collection of rigid capsules with a softer shader does not establish organic motion. Inspect the shoulder, hip, neck and limb bend under the intended action. Keep weight sums normalized and test the visible evaluated surface after reopening. Where rest components are fused/remeshed, transfer weights carefully and smooth them across connected topology; nearest-point transfer alone created jagged shoulder ridges in the local proof. Joint correction shapes may be needed for more extreme anatomy/poses.

Native Blender Preserve Volume uses quaternion skinning and can reduce joint collapse; it does not guarantee anatomical volume or collision-free results. Measure the relevant evaluated surface through the actual poses and inspect it. Shape keys, editable vertex deformations mixed by named values, can provide blinking, breathing and expressions. Keep their animation and feature attachments with the rig. Bone controls alone do not prove that eyes, glints, clothing or props follow correctly.

The task's `unified-skill/organic-animation-proof/` retains Moss: a continuous weighted creature body with flexible ears/tail, foot/hand targets and belly/blink keys. Read its README and receipt for the selected native master, exact deformation/volume/seek checks and final output. The volume measurement is a surface proxy for this short authored performance, not an anatomical benchmark. Native `.blend` is authoritative; do not promise equivalent volume-preserving deformation, constraints, shape keys or materials in another export without checking the receiver.

## Individually exposed drawings

When the brief requires frame-by-frame drawing, retain distinct drawings and an explicit exposure sheet, the mapping from output frames to held drawings. Author silhouette, line of action, breakdowns, smears, contacts and follow-through in the drawings themselves. Hold or replace whole drawings; choose interpolation only when the requested medium calls for it. Do not label cutout tweens, procedural jitter or cel-shaded3D as hand drawing.

The task's `unified-skill/drawn-animation-proof/` retains18original standalone SVG cels, a144frame sheet and actual6second1080p film. These are code-authored vector drawings in a drawn style, not human-hand-drawn art. Ordinary rebuilds preserve direct SVG edits; its `--author` mode deliberately regenerates the original art and must not be run over user revisions. Actual every-exposure boundary/reverse seeks and a one-cel expression revision are retained. For another project, check its own sequence and revised final output rather than treating these examples as a general validator.

Choose native Grease Pencil for editable strokes/3D integration or a dedicated raster drawing tool such as current Krita for brush-painted cels when that capability earns acquisition. Neither an SVG exposure proof nor a tool listing proves those native routes. Inspect their current API, asset terms and a representative edit/render before adopting one; no separate application is required for every drawn style.

## Acting beyond a valid rig

Begin with the particular thought, relationship and visible change, then block the action before smoothing everything. A face can have independent eye aim, upper/lower lids, brows, mouth corners and useful cheek/jaw controls. A drawn face may use new drawings instead. Keep these choices separate from software capability: more controls, motion capture or a better speech model do not choose a performance.

Let attention, face/head, torso and gesture have intentional timing rather than making every channel follow one scalar. Review eye lines from the actual camera. Use asymmetry, motivated blinks and holds when they help this character; fixed offsets or continuous idle motion are not rules. Check the partner's response and what prompts the decision, as well as the lead actor's pose. A technically attached hand can still communicate an unclear gesture.

For consequential acting, give a fresh reviewer the actual sequence before its intended emotion labels. Compare what they read with the brief, then revise concrete misreads. View the full film at normal speed and listen where an authorized route is available. When that is unavailable, label a still-sequence interpretation and technical checks honestly; neither establishes full-motion acting acceptance. Preserve the original draft and show that the affected performance changes, without claiming arbitrary cache reuse.

Independent pupil/lid/brow/mouth, head/body, scarf/hand, canopy and bird channels remain editable as native SVG and time functions. Blind initial image review read the intended change of mind and identified weak eye lines, gesture clarity, cause and partner response. Targeted revisions and actual encoded samples improve these; the original draft and unchanged opening pixels are retained. These are separate from interpretation and human normal-speed acceptance; use the README/receipt for precise scope.

For a sustained humanoid close-up, compare an appropriate ready rig against custom rigging before building anatomy from scratch. Native Blender5.1.2 already includes Rigify source with eye/lid/jaw controls, but its generation/fitting is not proven by that source inspection. Blender Studio's free CC-BY Snowv4 is a conditional ready-character option, not an installed or verified local dependency. Inspect embedded rig scripts, license/credit, selected release and local compatibility before execution. MPFB human expressions require the appropriate face assets. Capture/retargeting can supply motion, but needs authored eye lines, timing and contact correction. Choose for the actual cast and style rather than requiring one rig for all work.

## Speech-driven 2D mouth drawings

For local English speech-to-mouth cues, the retained Rhubarb1.14.0 nativearm64 CLI is exercised on this Mac. Its binary is at the original task's `work/unified-skill/character-research/build-arm64/rhubarb/rhubarb`; use that task's research/receipts to locate the exact resource folder and reproduce the patchedCLI-only build. No global installation or new TTS model is required. The official Intel binary crashed on the tested audio; nativearm64 completed. Do not silently select a different binary because its version command works.

Invoke the existing CLI directly, with fresh output, an English dialogue file matching the actual speech, and bounded CPU threads. Retain the audio and hashes, tool/build identity, raw JSON and command. Read `--help` from the actual binary for flags. Validate sorted finite contiguous cue intervals, legal mouth-shape labels, no overlap and final cue end against measured audio. Dialogue is a recognition aid, not proof that the audio spoke those words; use separate wording/listening review.

Map Rhubarb's A throughH and restX to deliberately drawn mouth variants. Use half-open intervals `[start,end)`, rest outside the spoken range, and explicit resets for every pose/mouth property on seek. Sample cue centers, boundaries, forward/reverse time and repeated times in the receiving renderer. Retiming audio requires regenerated or deliberately retimed cues. Phrase timestamps or Whisper draft captions are not viseme timing.
